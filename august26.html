⚠ Important Context - New App Launch on 14 August

FixMyScore launched a completely new app on 14 August. The user journey and conversion funnel changed completely, which affected how installs convert into payments. Prior to this, campaigns were optimised for Payment Success. After the launch, the goal given to BCM changed to driving 1,000 installs per day while keeping CPI under ₹20, focusing on install volume while the new funnel settled. The CAC increase from Week 3 onward is a direct result of this goal change. Both targets were met: 1,000+ installs per day and a monthly average CPI of ₹20. In the final week of August we changed the goal again, moving the optimisation objective back to Payment Success and CAC, which brought CAC down 52% week on week.

What Changed Each Week
Week 1 · Aug 1-7
Strong start on the existing funnel
Campaigns ran on the old app and proven funnel. CPI ₹30, CAC ₹242 with 256 payment successes. Install quality was good and payments converted consistently. This was the last full week on the old setup.
Week 2 · Aug 8-14
Transition week, new app goes live
CPI improved to ₹26. We scaled the App Install campaign in preparation for the launch. The new FixMyScore app went live on 14 August, changing the user journey and funnel from that point on.
Week 3 · Aug 15-22
New goal: 1,000 installs per day
With the new app live, the objective changed. BCM was tasked with driving 1,000 installs per day at a CPI under ₹20. We restructured campaigns across Google and Meta to deliver this. CPI dropped to ₹13 with 8,300 installs in the week. CAC rose because the new funnel had not settled for payment conversions yet.
Week 4 · Aug 23-31
Goal switched back to Payment Success
In the last week we changed the goal again, moving back to Payment Success and updating the campaign optimisation objective from App Installs to Payment Success and CAC. Campaigns were restructured for this. CAC improved from ₹1,958 in Week 3 to ₹947 in Week 4, a 52% reduction. New vernacular and UGC creatives were added to improve install quality and payment intent.

August in summary

The new app went live on 14 August and the funnel changed completely. The goal pivoted to 1,000 installs per day at a CPI under ₹20, and both targets were met. CPI improved 47% versus July, installs grew 131%. September moves back to CAC as the main metric, with a target of ₹250 and 10,000 payment successes on a ₹20L budget.

Meta Ads
1
Shift complete focus back to CAC optimisation. All campaigns will be restructured to optimise for Payment Success against a ₹250 CAC target. The App Install campaigns used through August will be retired or significantly reduced in budget allocation.
2
Up to 20 new creatives will be created and tested across 4 focused themes. Loan Resolution, Credit Score Improvement, Settlement and Loan Closure. Each theme will be made in multiple languages and tested in dedicated ad sets, not mixed into existing campaigns.
3
Establish the weekly UGC pipeline. We need 5 to 6 UGC videos per week from the FixMyScore team. UGC delivered our two best CAC results in August at ₹262 and ₹379, and will be key to hitting the September target.
Google Ads
4
Scale Google with 60% of the September budget (₹12L). Google delivered the volume in August at ₹15 CPI. With CAC now the primary metric, campaigns will be re-optimised toward Payment Success events. The larger budget helps the algorithm gather conversion data faster on the new funnel.
⚠ Action Required: Google Ads Account Funding

Please ensure sufficient budget is added to the Google Ads account prior to scaling. September pacing requires roughly ₹40,000 per day on Google, a significant step up from August. Given the aggressive scaling plan, if the budget runs out mid-month, delivery pauses, campaigns re-enter the learning phase, and both CAC and payment volume take a hit. Please keep the account funded ahead of each scaling step.

5
Continue Hinglish ad copies and expand to South Indian languages. Copies will be extended to Tamil, Telugu and Kannada in line with the app language rollout, keeping the problem-focused messaging that has worked so far.
