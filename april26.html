🎯 May 2026 — Insights & Action Plan

⚠️ April Key Concern — CVR & Payment Drop

While clicks surged +157% and CTR improved significantly (+83%), the click-to-install CVR dropped from ~43% (March) to 16% in April. Payment_success fell -18% with failed payments (2,053) exceeding successful ones (1,924). CAC rose to ₹270 (+15%). May focus must address these funnel inefficiencies.

Meta Ads
1
Fix the click-to-install CVR — April saw clicks double but CVR collapsed to 16%. Prioritise higher-intent audience segments over broad reach. Tighten targeting parameters on the creative testing campaign to improve post-click quality.
🎨 Creative Scaling Strategy — May '26

Scale April winners and introduce new formats to improve CVR:

✓ Scale Carousel 1 Gujarati (₹101 CAC — standout performer this month)
✓ Expand vernacular Carousels to Marathi, Tamil & Telugu based on Gujarati success
✓ Scale BCM_FMS_App promo 21/11 campaign (₹201 CAC, 60.3% CVR)
✓ Test new UGC & testimonial formats — trust signals to drive lower-funnel conversions
🗣️ Vernacular Campaign Expansion — May '26
✅ Currently running: Hindi, English & Gujarati (performing well)

In May, expand Carousel & Static formats to:

Marathi
Gujarati
Tamil SCALE
Telugu TEST

Carousel Gujarati (₹101 CAC) validates vernacular strategy — prioritise across all formats.

Google Ads
2
Increase Google budget allocation — Google continues to outperform Meta on CVR (25% vs 14%) and CAC (₹223 vs ₹280). BCM_FMS_ACI_Purchase delivered 3,080 installs at ₹29 CPI. Consider shifting 10–15% budget from Meta to Google in May to improve overall funnel efficiency and bring down blended CAC.
