Google vs Meta

Google: 54% of spend gave 73% of installs and 59% of Payment Success. CAC is ₹690, about 21% lower than Meta. Registration is easy (83% of installs), but only 4.98% of registrations pay.

Meta: CPI is ₹67 and CTR is 0.4%, so each install costs more. But 28.08% of registered users pay, nearly six times Google's rate. CAC lands at ₹869.

The gap to close: Google needs better payment conversion from its installs. Meta needs cheaper installs. Fixing either one moves overall CAC the most.

⚠ What the funnel shows

Payment failures are high. payment_failed fired 1,790 times across 848 users, against 1,420 payment_success events. Many failed users are retrying, so some of these people did want to pay.

App removals: app_remove events (18,304) are close to first_open events (21,867). These are not tracked as one group of users, so it is not an exact uninstall rate, but it shows a lot of users leave the app.

Reading these numbers: Google Analytics counts events from all sources, so its totals will not match the ad platforms' own Payment Success counts. The ad platform numbers are used for CAC in this report.
