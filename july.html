Week 1 High CPI & CAC, changes initiated
Week 1 started with high CPI (₹62) and CAC (₹480). We made immediate changes to campaign setup and improved ad messaging with new information tailored to the target audience. We also updated Google ad copies removing pricing points and shifting focus to problem statements around EMI issues, loan resolution, and CIBIL score improvement.
Week 2 Hinglish shift, Video testing, App Install campaign
We completely shifted to Hindi-based ads and copies to better reach Tier 2 India and communicate more effectively with the target audience. Messaging was repositioned around EMI issues, loan resolution, and how FMS can help improve long-term financial health placing FMS not just as an app but as a platform that genuinely helps users solve financial problems. We tested both static and video ads video ads performed significantly better. We also launched an App Install campaign optimised for installs, which helped bring CPI down.
Week 3 Retargeting tested, DPD cohorts planned
We launched a retargeting campaign and tested Lookalike audiences shared by the FMS team. Performance was not up to the mark, so we made the decision to pause these and instead plan a DPD-cohort based approach splitting audiences into DPD 1–60 days and DPD 61–365 days, with distinct messaging angles tailored to each cohort's financial situation and intent level.
Week 4 Stable CPI, strong end-of-month performance
By Week 4, CPI stabilised at ₹30 and CAC held at ₹257. Comparing July to the previous two months, performance improved meaningfully across CAC, CPI, and Registration-to-Payment-Success conversion. Video ads continued to deliver strong results. The App Install campaign maintained its efficiency, and the foundation for DPD cohort testing was set up for August.

🎯 August 2026 Insights & Action Plan

📋 July Overview Key Observations

July saw a strong recovery with CAC dropping 45% to ₹308, payment_success up 62%, and installs up 13%. The shift to Hinglish creatives, Hindi-first messaging, and the App Install campaign drove consistent week-on-week CPI improvement from ₹62 (W1) to ₹30 (W4). Retargeting and Lookalike campaigns were tested and paused in favour of a more structured DPD cohort-based approach.

Meta Ads
1
Launch DPD cohort-based retargeting campaigns Test two separate cohorts with tailored messaging: DPD 1–60 days (recent defaults, higher intent to resolve) and DPD 61–365 days (longer-standing issues, requiring a different communication angle). Each cohort will have distinct creative and copy strategies based on their financial situation and recovery stage.
2
Scale performing campaigns and test new creatives Scale based on week-on-week performance data. Continue testing new creative sets across different themes, maintaining the Hindi-first, problem-solution messaging that showed strong engagement in July.
🎨 Creative Testing Pipeline August '26

Building on July's learnings:

✓ Continue scaling video ad formats video significantly outperformed static in July
✓ Test new creative sets with fresh themes around EMI resolution, CIBIL improvement, and loan approval
✓ Maintain Hindi-first messaging for Tier 2 targeting position FMS as a long-term financial health partner
✓ Continue App Install campaign proved effective in reducing CPI week-on-week
Google Ads
3
Continue Hinglish ad copies and optimise based on performance The shift away from English to Hinglish copies, combined with problem-focused messaging (EMI issues, loan resolution, CIBIL improvement), has been effective. We will continue refining these copies and allocate budget across campaigns based on week-on-week performance data to further drive down CAC.
