🎯 July 2026 Insights & Action Plan

📋 June Overview Key Observations

CAC rose to ₹608 (+56%) in June. Two factors impacted performance this month: the Meta ad account was restricted in the 4th week of June, reducing delivery. Additionally, creative refreshes could not be executed on time due to delayed approvals, which affected CTR across campaigns. On Google, ad copies were updated and pricing information was removed to avoid attracting low-intent traffic.

Meta Ads
1
Monitor new campaigns on the new ad account and scale as per performance With the new Meta ad account now active, we will closely monitor campaign delivery and performance week-on-week and scale budgets on performing campaigns accordingly.
2
Test new sets of creatives based on different themes Launch a fresh creative pipeline in July with new content angles, revised themes, and formats to improve CTR and install quality across campaigns.
🎨 Creative Testing Pipeline July '26

New creative directions planned for July:

✓ Test new ad creatives based on fresh content themes and revised messaging angles
✓ Once vernacular video assets are received from the FMS team, test them in active campaigns across regional audiences
✓ Work on vernacular static creatives in parallel to expand regional language coverage
Google Ads
3
Update Google ad copies to Hinglish and optimise based on performance Ad copies will be revised from English to Hinglish to better connect with the target audience and improve click quality. Budget will be allocated across campaigns based on week-on-week performance data.
